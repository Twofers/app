What happens after approval

This approval starts one visible nine-step checklist. The operations console will show exactly where the owner needs help.

  1. 1Application approved
  2. 2Owner email verified
  3. 3Business information complete
  4. 4Business terms accepted
  5. 5Trial or access activated
  6. 6Billing confirmed
  7. 7First offer created
  8. 8First offer published
  9. 9Redemption tested

The owner signs in to the app with this email to claim the approved business and finish setup. No trial, credit, claim, or Stripe access is granted until Checkout activation.
