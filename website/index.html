Frequently asked questions

What is Twofer?

Twofer is a live local deals app for customers and an AI-assisted promotion tool for local businesses.

Are the deals real?

Yes. Deals in the app come from approved local businesses. The app screens and cards on this page show sample offers.

Why do businesses need to be approved?

New business accounts are reviewed to keep published deals tied to real local businesses.

What kinds of promotions can businesses create?

Two-for-one deals, bonus items, and limited-time discounts, each with a controlled time window, quantity, and terms.

Is Twofer free for customers?

Yes. Twofer is free for customers.

Where is Twofer available?

Twofer is live in the Dallas-Fort Worth area, with more cities planned.
